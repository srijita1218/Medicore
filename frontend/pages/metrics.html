INTERPRETATION

The AMQ scheduler prioritizes time-critical Emergency workloads while allowing Interactive processes to share CPU time through Round Robin scheduling. Background workloads use FCFS and may be promoted through aging when they experience prolonged waiting.

In the simulated hospital workload, AMQ reduces average waiting and turnaround time compared with the FCFS baseline while providing immediate response for Emergency processes.

Metrics shown above are based on the current hospital workload simulation.
